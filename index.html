Our Culinary Heritage

A Roadside Sanctuary with Bengali Spirit

"Every dish carries the unconditional warmth of a mother’s touch, simmered with hand-pounded spices on slow flame."

150+ Local Mothers Employed
50,000 Sq. Ft. Campus Area

Established along National Highway 12 in Krishnanagar, Mother’s Hut is Bengal’s premier highway gastronomic destination. Founded on a revolutionary philosophy: empowering local mothers to cook generational recipes in a state-of-the-art facility without compromising on handmade authenticity.

Here, you choose the tempo of your journey. Settle into the sepia calm of our 1920s Chowringhee colonial hall for artisanal sweets and Darjeeling chai, immerse in the terracotta warmth of Basudha, or relax under the open skies in Bagicha. Hot, fragrant delicacies are prepared fresh and brought directly to your table — often escorted by Nadia’s first autonomous AI server robot.
